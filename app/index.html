Highest balance of your bank accounts, for the FBAR

Add CIH or Attijariwafa monthly statements, or a BMCE transaction-history PDF. This page calculates each account’s highest balance during the year and converts it to US dollars. A BMCE history requires a dated balance before its result can be final.

Your statements stay on this computer. Everything happens inside this page, on your computer. Nothing is uploaded or sent anywhere, and this page is built so that it cannot connect to the internet at all. To be completely sure, you can turn off Wi-Fi before adding your statements.

A one-page-per-account report with the numbers for the FBAR form, how they were found, and any problems. The detailed version adds every transaction of every statement, to check against your PDFs. It opens your browser’s print window: to keep it as a file, choose “Save as PDF” as the printer (on Windows: “Microsoft Print to PDF”), or print it for your tax preparer.

Optional, for checking the details: spreadsheet files (CSV, open in Excel or Numbers) with every statement and every transaction, including the balance after each one.

These files are saved on this computer only. They contain your account numbers and balances: keep them private.

What to do with this number

  • The FBAR is FinCEN Form 114, filed online through BSA E-Filing. For each account, the “number for the FBAR form” shown above goes in Part II, item 15, “Maximum value of account during calendar year”. One entry per account.
  • For the same account you will also need: item 16, type of account: Bank; item 17, the institution name shown in the report; item 18, the account number without spaces; and items 19 to 23, the bank’s address. These details appear with each account’s results.
  • An FBAR is required if the maximums of all your foreign accounts, added together, are more than $10,000, even if each maximum happened on a different date. Once you are over that amount, every foreign account must be listed, even the small ones.
  • Using your statements this way is accepted: FinCEN allows the maximum value to be taken from periodic account statements, as long as they fairly reflect it. This page is more precise than month-end balances: it checks the balance after every transaction on every statement.
  • You may also need IRS Form 8938 with your tax return if your foreign assets are larger (thresholds start at $50,000); ask your tax preparer.
  • If you are unsure whether you need to file, or how to fill it in, check with your tax preparer.

If a statement can’t be read

  • Use the PDF downloaded from your bank’s online banking. A scan or photo has no text this page can read.
  • Password-protected PDF: open it with its password, choose Print, then “Save as PDF”, and add the new copy.
  • Something else is wrong: next to the file, click “Create a safe diagnostic file”. It saves a small text file that shows only the layout of the statement: every letter is replaced by “x” and every digit by “9”, so it contains no names, no account numbers and no amounts. You can send that file to the person who made this tool so they can fix it.
